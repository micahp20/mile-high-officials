Youth sports referee recruiting

There’s a nationwide shortage of youth sports referees.

Mile High Officials is recruiting youth sports referees, umpires, and officials across Colorado. If you love sports, want to give back, and are ready to lead with confidence, this is a real opportunity. Officials are paid for the games they work.

Sports officials play a vital role in shaping the quality of youth athletic programs. It is a challenging job, but when handled the right way, it is rewarding, respected, and deeply connected to the sports you love.

Become a youth sports official Email admin@milehighofficials.com

All sports need officials

Basketball, baseball, football, soccer, softball, and more all depend on trained youth sports referees and umpires.

Flexible work

Officiating can fit around school, work, family life, and other responsibilities while keeping you close to sports.

Real growth

Officials build confidence, communication, decision-making, and leadership under pressure.

Why this matters

Officials shape the game experience.

01

Instant decision-making

Sports officials must make fast judgments, stay calm, and manage game situations with professionalism and authority.

02

Conflict resolution

Referees and umpires help keep games organized by communicating clearly and resolving tension in real time.

03

Fitness and focus

Depending on the sport, officials need awareness, mobility, and the ability to keep up with the action from start to finish.

Who can become an official

You don’t need a perfect background. You need the right mindset.

  • Former athletes, ex-competitors, parents, teachers, students, and fans can all become youth sports referees.
  • You can start by learning the rules of the sport, attending clinics, and getting experience at the local level.
  • From there, officials can advance into high school and college sports, and in some cases pursue elite-level opportunities.
  • What matters most is professionalism, consistency, communication, and a real passion for officiating.

Why join Mile High Officials

Training, opportunity, and a path forward.

01

Learn the role

Get guidance on rules, mechanics, professionalism, and the real expectations of working youth sports events.

02

Stay in sports

Keep your connection to the games you love while helping young athletes compete in a structured environment.

03

Build a future

Officiating can begin as part-time work and grow into a serious long-term lane for skilled, dependable officials.
