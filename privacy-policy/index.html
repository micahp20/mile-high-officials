Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

This policy explains what information Mile High Officials Inc collects through this website and how we use it. Effective November 13, 2026.

Who we are

Mile High Officials Inc is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 45-5183098) located at 150 Wells St #582, Erie, CO 80516. We recruit, train, mentor, and assign sports officials for youth and amateur sports in Colorado.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the information you enter: your name, email address, phone number (optional), and message. If you email us, call us, or mail us a check, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, and gift amount.

This website does not require an account and does not take online payments.

How we use your information

We use the information you send only to respond to your inquiry, such as answering questions about becoming an official, assigning services, or donations, and to acknowledge gifts we receive.

Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Contact form messages are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, a form delivery service, and this website is hosted by Vercel. These providers handle data only to deliver the website and your message. We may disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website saves your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives standard request information such as your IP address. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs. If we add analytics or advertising measurement tools in the future, we will update this policy before doing so.

Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Young people interested in officiating should contact us with a parent or guardian.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you have sent us at any time by emailing admin@milehighofficials.com.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email admin@milehighofficials.com, call 720-432-0354, or write to Mile High Officials Inc, 150 Wells St #582, Erie, CO 80516.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
